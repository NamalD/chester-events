# Local event sources

Checked **6 September 2026**. Linked pages are the evidence for each entry. Most are organiser or venue pages; third-party evidence and inaccessible accounts are labelled explicitly. Collection methods are recommendations unless described as tested in [verified collection routes](verified-collection-routes.md).

**Priority:** P1 = first collection set; P2 = useful expansion; P3 = occasional or conditional lead. Priorities reflect this app’s interests, expected distinctiveness, and collection effort, not measured event volume. “No API verified” does not mean no API exists. Refresh suggestions appear in the collection plan.

## Stale leads to avoid importing blindly

- **ōH Chester Design Foundation’s former shop/workshop:** an [Eco Communities article from November 2025](https://ecocommunitiesuk.com/2025/11/04/sustainable-fashion-with-roots-in-chester-showcasing-oscar-munroe-alena-kudera/) reports its loss, despite old articles and recommendations still circulating. Keep any new designer activity as a fresh organiser lead; do not seed the old venue as active.
- **Jaunty Goat:** relevant to vegan discovery, but the [pop-up collaboration article](https://www.jauntygoat.co.uk/jaunty-goat-at-the-openhome-chester-pop-up/) is historical. A vegan-friendly café is not automatically an event source; require an actual current programme.
- Recurring dance listings, festival landing pages and shop-based tickets are particularly prone to carrying outdated locations or dates. A page being reachable is not sufficient evidence of a future event.
